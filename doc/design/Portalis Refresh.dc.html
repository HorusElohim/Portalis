Revisiting the built app
Three structural fixes to what your eng shipped. One: the sidebar's magnet field, Paste/Add buttons and duplicate icon buttons are gone — replaced by a single paste-or-search bar at the top of every page that accepts an invite key, a magnet link, an info hash, or a search term. The sidebar becomes pure navigation. Two: the empty right-hand canvas is now a two-column workspace, so pages have a rail of live context instead of 900px of black. Three: People and collection detail come back on mobile, and every empty state does a job instead of apologising.
Not in the build yet — needs a spec before it goes to engineering
Two things below are new product surface, not a restyle of what exists: the desktop Activity feed (needs an event model — what gets logged, retention, whether it is per-collection or global) and the mobile Nearby right now list (needs a local-discovery + trust-prompt flow) and the mobile This week stats chart (needs a metrics store — what is counted, per-day buckets, how long history is kept). All are marked in place. When either one goes into a dev prompt, ask me first so we can write the UX properly rather than shipping the mock.
